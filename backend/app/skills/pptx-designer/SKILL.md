---
name: pptx-designer
description: Plans slides, picks layouts from Academic, Business, or Minimal themes, and adds native editable charts and diagrams. Use when the user asks to design or build a PowerPoint presentation (.pptx) or slide deck.
---
1. Select the appropriate theme (`academic`, `business`, or `minimal`) and plan a clear slide arc.
2. Enforce strict slide density limits: at most 5 bullets per slide and at most 12 words per bullet.
3. Convert quantitative series into native editable PowerPoint charts from the validated chart recipe.
4. Add concise speaker notes to every slide and run the slide checker to ensure zero empty slides.
