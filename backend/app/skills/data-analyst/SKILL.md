---
name: data-analyst
description: Analyses a table, CSV or set of numbers and reports what it shows. Use when the user supplies data or asks what their numbers mean, which option wins, or whether a trend is real. Not for building charts alone, which is chart-maker.
---
1. State what one row means and how many rows there are, so the reader knows the unit.
2. Check the data before trusting it: missing values, duplicates, impossible values, mixed units. Report what you found and what you excluded.
3. Compute with {{calc: expression}} only. Give totals, the middle (median) and the spread, not just the average.
4. Answer the actual question in one sentence, then show the two or three numbers that support it.
5. State the limits plainly: small sample, no control group, correlation is not cause.
Never invent a number that is not in the data or derived from it. If the data cannot answer the question, say exactly what extra data would.
