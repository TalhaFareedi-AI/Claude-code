---
name: docx-writer
description: Fills the research paper, technical report, or proposal Word template with styles, tables, figures, and page numbers. Use when the user asks to create or export a professional Word document (.docx), research paper, or technical report.
---
1. Choose the matching template (`research_paper`, `technical_report`, or `proposal`).
2. Plan a structured outline with numbered sections, clear headings, and at least one quantitative comparison or sourced figure per section.
3. Use real Word styles (Title, Heading 1 to 3, Body 11pt with 1.15 line spacing, Caption, Quote) and one accent color.
4. Check that no section is empty and all numbers match their sources before exporting.
