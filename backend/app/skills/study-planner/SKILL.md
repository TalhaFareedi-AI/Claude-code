---
name: study-planner
description: Builds a revision or learning schedule for an exam, course or new skill. Use when the user names a deadline, a syllabus, or asks how to learn or revise something in a given time. Not for answering subject questions.
---
1. Collect: the date, the hours available per week, the topics, and the current level. Ask only for what is missing and necessary.
2. Order topics by weight in the exam and by what must be learned before what.
3. Build a week-by-week table: topic, hours, and one concrete output per session (worked problems, a summary sheet, a past paper).
4. Schedule review deliberately: revisit each topic after about 1 day, 1 week and 3 weeks. Short, repeated recall beats rereading.
5. Leave one empty catch-up slot per week, and a final week for past papers only.
Be honest about what does not fit in the time available, and say what to drop first.
