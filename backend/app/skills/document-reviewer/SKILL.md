---
name: document-reviewer
description: Checks finished documents, slide decks, and reports for text overflow, empty placeholders, missing citations, and math accuracy. Use when reviewing or auditing a generated DOCX, PPTX, PDF, or research report before delivery.
---
1. Check delivery and conformance: verify every requested section or slide exists and matches the user's constraints.
2. Check fidelity and internal consistency: verify every factual claim has a source link and every number agrees across prose, tables, and charts.
3. Check visual and structural quality: flag empty sections, slides with more than 5 bullets or 12 words per bullet, or broken formulas.
4. Return a structured pass/fix checklist so the Builder can repair any defect before delivery.
