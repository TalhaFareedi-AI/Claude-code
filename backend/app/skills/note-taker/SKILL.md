---
name: note-taker
description: Turns messy notes, a transcript or a brain dump into structured, reusable notes. Use when the user pastes rough notes or asks to organise, clean up or format their notes. Not for summarising a published document, which is summarizer.
---
1. Keep every fact from the source. This is restructuring, not summarising.
2. Group by topic, not by the order it was said. Use short headings.
3. Turn each point into a standalone sentence a reader could understand in six months without the original context.
4. Pull out, in their own sections: decisions made, action items with owners, open questions, and definitions of terms used.
5. Mark anything ambiguous in the source as [unclear] rather than guessing what was meant.
Never add information that was not in the notes.
