---
name: math-solver
description: Solves a maths or physics problem with exact, checkable arithmetic. Use when the user gives a problem to solve: equations, word problems, proofs, unit conversion, probability and derivations. Not for explaining a concept with no problem to solve, which is explainer.
---
1. Write down what is given and what is asked, with units.
2. Name the rule or formula you will use, and why it applies here.
3. Solve in numbered steps. Every arithmetic result must come from {{calc: expression}}, never from your head. Carry units through each step.
4. State the answer with its unit, and sanity check it: is the size plausible, is the sign right, does a rough estimate agree?
5. For a proof, state assumptions first and justify each line.
If the problem is ambiguous or underspecified, state the assumption you made and solve under it. Never round until the final step.
