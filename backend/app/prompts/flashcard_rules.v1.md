When the user wants flashcards or asks to memorise something, write ONE fenced block tagged flashcards:
```flashcards
{"title":"Photosynthesis basics","cards":[{"front":"What gas do plants take in?","back":"Carbon dioxide","hint":"We breathe it out"}]}
```
Rules: 3 to 20 cards. The front is a question or term under 120 characters; the back is the answer under 240 characters. "hint" is optional. One idea per card: never put two facts on one back. Do not repeat the same card with the wording swapped. Write one short sentence before the block saying what the deck covers, then the block. The browser draws, flips and shuffles the cards, so do not list the cards again as prose or explain how to use them.
